## About the course

Biophysics introduces the physical principles underlying the structure and function of the human body. During the course you will learn how physical laws explain biological processes and how they are applied in modern medical diagnostics and therapy.

The course combines lectures, seminars and laboratory classes, allowing students to understand both theoretical concepts and their practical applications.

---

## What will you learn?

After completing the course you will be able to:

* explain the physical principles governing cells, tissues and organs,
* understand how physical factors influence the human body,
* describe membrane transport and bioelectrical phenomena,
* understand the physical basis of medical imaging techniques,
* explain the interaction of ionizing radiation with matter and living tissues,
* understand the principles of radiation protection,
* perform simple laboratory measurements and analyse experimental results,
* interpret basic physiological signals such as ECG,
* understand the principles of scientific measurement and data analysis.

---

## Course topics

Topics covered during the course include:

### Lectures

* thermodynamics of biological systems,
* molecular interactions and biological membranes,
* membrane transport,
* bioelectricity and action potentials,
* magnetic resonance imaging (MRI),
* effects of temperature and humidity on the human body,
* non-ionizing radiation,
* physical principles of diagnostic imaging.

### Laboratory classes

* radioactive decay and radioisotopes,
* interaction of ionizing radiation with matter,
* radiation detection and measurement,
* biophysics of human voice,
* electrocardiography (ECG),
* arterial blood pressure measurements,
* spectrophotometry,
* polarimetry,
* refractometry,
* optical imaging,
* polarization of light,
* computer-assisted analysis of biomedical signals,
* laser beam attenuation.

### Seminars

* introduction to mathematical methods used in biophysics.

## Course assessment

The course consists of:

* lectures,
* seminars,
* laboratory classes,
* final examination.

To be admitted to the final exam students must:

* attend all required classes,
* complete all laboratory classes,
* obtain the required number of points from laboratory work and the colloquium.

Students who achieve the required threshold during the course may be exempt from the final examination according to the current course regulations.
