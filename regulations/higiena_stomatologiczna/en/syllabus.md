## About the course

The course combines the fundamentals of biochemistry and biophysics, helping you understand processes in the human body at both molecular and physical levels. The biochemistry part presents the structure and metabolism of the human body, with emphasis on knowledge useful in further studies. The biophysics part covers physical and biophysical foundations of life processes, environmental factors, and the physical basis of diagnostic and therapeutic methods.

---

## What will you learn?

After completing the course you will be able to:

### Biochemistry

- distinguish the structure of amino acids, nucleotides, monosaccharides, carboxylic acids and their derivatives that form macromolecules,
- identify enzymes involved in digestion,
- describe basic reactions of organic and inorganic compounds in aqueous solutions,
- predict the direction of biochemical processes,
- describe changes in body function when homeostasis is disturbed.

### Biophysics

- explain how ionizing radiation interacts with the body and how it is used for therapeutic and diagnostic purposes,
- assess the harmful effects of radiation dose,
- understand basic principles of radiological protection for staff and patients,
- explain the physicochemical basis of the senses (hearing and vision),
- describe the physical foundations of non-invasive and invasive imaging methods,
- apply laws of physics to cell biology, tissues and physiological processes,
- explain how external factors such as temperature, gravity, pressure, electromagnetic fields and ionizing radiation affect the human body.

---

## Course topics

### Biochemistry (lectures and laboratory classes)

- amino acids, peptides, proteins,
- carbohydrates and lipids,
- nucleotides and nucleic acids,
- enzymology,
- bioenergetics and metabolic regulation.

### Biophysics (lectures)

- electrical phenomena in cells and tissues,
- basics of electrocardiography,
- biophysical foundations of hearing,
- physical properties of sound,
- ultrasound and its medical applications,
- heat exchange between the body and the environment; thermoregulation,
- effects of visible light, ultraviolet and infrared radiation on the body,
- effects of ionizing radiation on the body; radiation protection.

## Organisation of classes

The course is divided into two parts:

## Course assessment

### Biochemistry

- attendance at all classes (one absence allowed),

- no separate final test – pass based on attendance and work during classes.
