# Rules and Regulations for the Biophysics Course

## Basic Information

* **Faculty:** Faculty of Medicine, Medical University of Białystok
* **Programme:** English Division (Medicine)

---

# Course Organization

Attendance is mandatory for all forms of classes, both on-site and online.

The course consists of:

* **4 seminars**, conducted according to the schedule published by the Department of Medical Biophysics,
* **10 hours of online lectures**,
* **9 laboratory classes**, divided into three thematic modules (3 experiments in each module).

Laboratory classes take place in the Department of Medical Biophysics.

---

# Student Responsibilities

Before attending laboratory classes, students are expected to:

* prepare the required theoretical material,
* keep the **Biophysics Handbook** up to date,
* wear a clean laboratory coat,
* bring appropriate indoor footwear.

---

# Attendance Policy

* Unexcused absences are not permitted.
* Absences must be justified within **7 days** after the reason for the absence has ended.
* Acceptable justification includes:

  * a medical certificate,
  * Dean's leave,
  * a documented unforeseen event.
* Missed classes must be completed on a date agreed with the instructor.
* Arriving more than **15 minutes late** is treated as an absence.

---

# Laboratory Assessment

Each laboratory exercise is passed by:

* demonstrating theoretical preparation,
* correctly performing the experiment,
* analysing the obtained results,
* completing the laboratory report in the **Biophysics Handbook**.

Completion of each exercise is confirmed by the instructor's signature.

Failed laboratory exercises must be repeated before the colloquium.

---

# Bonus Points

Students may receive:

* up to **18 bonus points** for activity and outstanding performance during laboratory classes (2 points per exercise),
* up to **2 additional points** for preparing a short educational video promoting the use of physics in medicine.

---

# Colloquium

Students may take the colloquium after completing all laboratory classes and attending (or making up) all seminars and lectures.

The colloquium consists of **50 multiple-choice questions**:

* 30 questions from laboratory topics,
* 20 questions from lecture material.

Each correct answer is worth **1 point**.

The final colloquium score includes:

* 50 points from the colloquium,
* 18 bonus points from laboratory classes,
* 2 bonus points for the educational video.

The maximum possible score is **70 points**.

---

# Admission to the Final Exam

A minimum of **30 points** is required to qualify for the final examination.

Students scoring below 30 points may retake the colloquium **twice**.

Students who achieve **56 points or more** in the first colloquium are exempt from the final examination and receive the final grade **5 (Very Good)**.

An unexcused absence from the colloquium results in **0 points**.

---

# Final Examination

The final examination consists of:

* multiple-choice questions,
* open-ended questions,
* calculation problems.

Students are entitled to:

* one regular examination,
* two retake examinations.

### Grading Scale

| Exam score | Grade |
| --------: | :---: |
| 0–59% | 2 (Fail) |
| 60–68% | 3 (Satisfactory) |
| 69–76% | 3+ (Satisfactory Plus) |
| 77–84% | 4 (Good) |
| 85–90% | 4+ (Good Plus) |
| 91–100% | 5 (Very Good) |

Failure to attend an examination without valid justification results in a failing grade.

---

# Results and Review

* Examination and colloquium results are published within **14 days** on the Blackboard platform.
* Students have the right to review their written work at a time agreed upon with the Department of Medical Biophysics.

---

# Academic Integrity

Any student found using unauthorized assistance during a colloquium or examination will:

* have the examination terminated,
* receive **0 points / a failing grade**,
* lose the right to retake the assessment.

Detailed regulations are defined in the UMB Study Regulations and the Rector's Ordinance.

---

# Final Provisions

In matters not covered by these regulations, the Study Regulations of the Medical University of Białystok shall apply.
