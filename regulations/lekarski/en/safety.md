# Occupational health and safety training (OHS)

**Rules of conduct for students during Medical Biophysics laboratory classes.**

1. Students must follow the OHS rules presented during the induction training for new students at UMB.
2. Before entering the laboratory, students must put on a **clean** lab coat and change footwear.
3. Only students completing the laboratory exercise and the instructor may be present in the room. A student may leave only with the instructor's permission.
4. Students may perform only the activities instructed by the teacher.
5. Move carefully around the laboratory at all times.
6. Maintain silence, calm, and order in teaching rooms.
7. Smoking (including e-cigarettes), eating, drinking, chewing gum, and using mobile phones are strictly forbidden in laboratory and lecture rooms.
8. Only essential instruments and materials may be placed on laboratory benches.
9. Any irregularity or unexpected event (e.g. spilled reagent, damaged equipment) must be reported to the instructor immediately.
10. Removing any reagents, preparations, or radioactive samples from the laboratory is forbidden.
11. All reagents must be taken using automatic pipettes.
12. Equipment may be operated only under the instructor's supervision or with explicit permission, following the operating instructions for each device.
13. Students must not attempt to repair equipment on their own.
