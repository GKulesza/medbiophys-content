# Regulations for teaching classes in biophysics

## Faculty of Medicine of the Medical University of Lublin, Faculty of Medicine

1. Student attendance is mandatory at all forms of classes (conducted stationary and online).

2. How to conduct classes:
   - a. Biophysics classes are held in the form of lectures, seminars and laboratory exercises.
   - b. Teaching classes begin with a series of four seminars, which are held in seminar groups in accordance with the schedule available on the website of the Department of Medical Biophysics.
   - c. Lectures on biophysics (10 hours) are conducted online in accordance with the schedule available on the website of the Department of Medical Biophysics.
   - d. Laboratory exercises in biophysics are divided into 3 thematic sections, 3 exercises for each section (9 exercises in total), which take place in the building of the Medical Biophysics Department of the Medical University of Lublin in laboratory groups. A detailed schedule of classes can be found on the website of the Department of Medical Biophysics.

3. Before entering the exercise room, students are required to put on a clean apron and change their shoes.

4. Unexcused absences are not allowed during classes.

5. Absences from classes are justified on the basis of a medical certificate, leave/dean's leave or a declaration of an accidental accident, and the justification should be delivered to the head of the unit within 7 days from the end of the circumstances justifying the absence. Absences from classes must be made up. The date and method of making up classes is agreed individually with the teacher.

6. Lateness of more than 15 minutes is considered an absence from classes.

7. Laboratory exercises, seminars and lectures end with a colloquium, which can be taken by students who have passed all exercises and participated in all lectures and seminars or have made up for any absences.
   - a. To pass the exercise, the student must demonstrate theoretical knowledge of the prepared material, correctly conduct the experiment by the student (or team), analyze the results and present them together with conclusions in the form of a report in the "Biophysics Exercise Book". Completing an exercise is confirmed by the teacher's signature in the appropriate place in the table under a given exercise.
   - b. If you fail the exercise, you must correct it before the test date.
   - c. During each exercise, a student can obtain a maximum of 2 additional points for activity and demonstrating above-average knowledge (18 points in total).
   - d. It is possible to obtain a maximum of 2 additional points during lectures for creating a short film popularizing the use of physics in medicine (details on the topic and form are available from the lecturer).
   - e. The colloquium is conducted in the form of a test consisting of 50 closed questions of the ABCD type (10 questions from each of the three training sections and 20 questions regarding the content presented during the lectures). You can get 1 point for each correctly solved task.

8. The final grade of the student's work is the sum of additional points and points obtained during the colloquium. The maximum number of points that can be obtained is the sum of points obtained during the colloquium (50 points), additional points obtained during laboratory exercises (18 points), additional points obtained during lectures (video) (2 points) and is 70 points.

9. The condition for admission to the exam is to obtain a total of at least 30 points.

10. If the final grade is less than 30 points, the student has the right to re-take the test twice. Re-sit tests are conducted in the same form as the first colloquium. If the final grade after additional tests is less than 30 points, the student is not allowed to take the exam.

11. Students who obtained 56 points or more after the first test are exempt from the exam and receive a very good grade.

12. Unexcused absence from the colloquium results in obtaining 0 credit points.

13. Absences from the colloquium are justified on the basis of a medical certificate, leave/dean's leave or a declaration of an accidental accident, and the justification should be delivered to the head of the unit within 7 days from the end of the circumstances justifying the absence.

14. The entire course is assessed in the form of an exam. The exam on the first date and two resit dates is conducted in the form of a test consisting of closed and open questions (descriptive and arithmetical questions).

15. Depending on the number of points obtained in the exam, the student receives the following grade:

| Percentage of exam marks | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| 0 - 59 | 2 |
| 60 - 68 | 3 |
| 69 - 76 | 3+ |
| 77 - 84 | 4 |
| 85 - 90 | 4+ |
| 91 - 100 | 5 |

16. The student is entitled to two make-up exams. Make-up exams are conducted in the same form as the first exam.

17. Failure to appear for an exam/sit-up examination without justification will result in receiving an unsatisfactory grade.

18. The rules for justifying absences from exams and make-up exams are specified in the Study Regulations.

19. Exam dates (basic and 2 resit exams) are set by the Department of Medical Biophysics in consultation with the student representative. It is permissible to appoint an additional one for all persons who justified their absence on any of the previous dates. This deadline will take place in March (no later than March 31). No additional exam dates other than those set out above are scheduled.

20. The results of the assessment/examination are communicated to the student within 14 days from the date of the assessment/examination. The results are made available in a file available in the course content on the Blackboard platform.

21. The student has the right to inspect his/her written work on the date set by the Department of Medical Biophysics and the starosta of the year in the presence of the head of the unit or a person designated by him.

22. If a student is found to have or use an unauthorized form of assistance, the student's colloquium/exam will be interrupted, which is equivalent to receiving 0 points/unsatisfactory grade with no possibility of improvement. Detailed provisions on prohibited assistance are included in the Study Regulations and the Rector's Order.

23. In matters not regulated by these Regulations, the provisions of the Regulations for First Cycle, Second Cycle and Uniform Master's Studies of the Medical University of Bialystok apply.
